# Sync Pro Brand Guidelines

## Positioning
Sync Pro is a premium operations workspace for teams that need clear ownership, clean status visibility, and fast decision-making.

## Voice
- Clear, direct, and calm.
- Prefer familiar labels: Tasks, People, Notifications, Settings.
- Avoid internal jargon when a user-facing word is clearer.

## Typography
- Use Poppins only.
- Use strong weight for headings, medium weight for navigation, and regular or medium weight for body text.
- Use small uppercase labels only for metadata.

## Layout
- Use flex for compact rows and alignment.
- Use grid for dashboards, card collections, and two-column settings panels.
- Keep component padding `em` based and page widths responsive.
- Desktop uses a fixed sidebar; mobile uses bottom navigation.

## Motion
- Use motion sparingly.
- Allowed regions: dashboard hero entry and AI drawer open/close.
- Avoid animated cards, notification rows, and repeated list items.

## Color
- Primary: Indigo `#4f46e5`.
- Surface: White `#ffffff`.
- Background: Slate `#f8fafc`.
- Text: Slate `#0f172a`.
- Success, warning, and error colors are reserved for status signals only.
